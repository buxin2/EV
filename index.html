🇬🇲 Gambian Innovator & Entrepreneur

Turning ideas into technology

I'm Abdoukadir Jabbi, known as Abdo: a Gambian technology entrepreneur, inventor and computer scientist, driven by using technology to solve real-world problems.

My journey began with curiosity about how machines work and how technology can improve lives. Instead of only learning in theory, I started building, exploring electronics, programming and robotics, and turning ideas into working projects.

Growing up in The Gambia, I wanted Africans not only to use technology made elsewhere, but to design, manufacture and develop their own. That vision led me to start BuXin on 12 April 2023, and in August 2023 I began my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Applications at Sharda University in India.

My mission: turn ideas into technology, technology into solutions, and solutions into opportunities for Africa.

🎓 Computer Science, Sharda University🤖 Founder, AiDoBot🏢 Founder, BuXin

MARKET CONTEXT

Africa's electric market is early, and moving fast

~40%

Year-on-year growth in electric two-wheeler sales in Africa, reaching 9,000 vehicles in 2024 (a 0.5% share of two-wheeler sales).

2×+

Electric car sales in Africa more than doubled to nearly 11,000 in 2024, with sales shares under 1%.

Top

UNEP notes motorcycle growth rates in many African countries are some of the highest in the world.

Our reading: a low electric share today leaves open ground for an affordable, locally built entrant.

Sources: IEA, Global EV Outlook 2025 (pp. 20, 60); UNEP, Electric two and three-wheelers.
